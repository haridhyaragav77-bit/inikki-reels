# daily-brief
data/brief.md is written by the Engine before each run: what to do, Harish's standing rules and ideas, today's
slots, top 5 trends, yesterday's rates with colours, changes in force, bank level, budgets, alerts, new feedback.
Start from it. Open another file only when the brief points to it. Do not read raw metrics or old plans.
Harish's standing rules come first: follow them in every plan. Your Control Room settings are in data/control/.
Notes meant for Harish go to data/out/ (digest.md, weekly_report.md, monthly_review.md, alerts.md); the Engine
puts them in Drive.
