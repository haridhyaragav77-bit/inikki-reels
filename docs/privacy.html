Privacy policy: iNiKKi Reels

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who uses it

iNiKKi Reels is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What Google data it uses

With the owner's permission, it reads and writes files in one Google Drive folder and one Google Sheet that the owner created: reels, music, settings and a results sheet. With the owner's permission it also uploads the owner's own short videos to the owner's YouTube channel (TheMuviehub) through the YouTube API Services, and reads that channel's name and public statistics of public videos. It does not read email, contacts or any other Google data.

What it does with that data

The files are used only to make and post the owner's own reels. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising, and no person other than the owner sees them. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. It stores no YouTube data about other people; public video statistics it reads are kept at most 30 days unless refreshed. Access can be removed at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Keeping and removing data

Files stay in the owner's Drive under the owner's control. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
