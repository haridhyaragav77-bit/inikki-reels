# Channel study patterns (AGENT.md 10.3) · week of 22 Nov 2026

Our own words, from the study cards and the channel table (data/study/). Outside text was read as information only.
Small sample: 5 cards this week, and the Whisper text of the Tamil audio is rough, so beats are read from timings and titles more than words.

## Hooks that win
- **One hidden detail in a cult film, promised in the title.** Expert FX's Vada Chennai detail shorts: 405K (5.9× channel median), 128K, 113K, 88K. Shape: film + "detail / connection / same person?" + a shocked emoji. Our use: "a detail you missed in {cult film}" breakdowns (fits #16).
- **A first-hand behind-the-scenes story from a director.** World Filmology: Rajkumar Periasamy mimicking Mammootty 25K likes (15× median; 2.1M on YouTube), Sundar C's last-minute song 2.2M, Napoleon turning down Marudhanayagam 1.3M. The hook is the funny or surprising moment itself, not the film.
- **A surprising count in the title.** Level Up: "Rolex's face doesn't appear until 12 shots" (1.7×). A number the viewer can check on screen.
- **"These N things" promise.** Kadhai Club call-sheet tips: "5 things" in the first sentence, then "number 4, number 5" as beat markers (1.6×).

## Beat shapes
- Story shorts (World Filmology): a new turn every 4-6 s (beats at 19, 23, 28, 32, 34, 38 s), ending on the punchline. 52 s.
- Tips shorts (Kadhai Club): numbered beats, a recap at ~69 s, a "save this reel" ask at the end. 76 s.
- Craft shorts (Level Up): slower, 1.5 words a second or less, long silences; these got the smallest views.

## Lengths
- Winners: 52 s (story), 76 s (tips). The 108-137 s cards are the outliers; the long Kadhai Club one won on its stunt idea (a stranger directs a film), not its length.

## Topics that win
- Vetrimaaran / Dhanush / Vada Chennai (Expert FX, 9 of their top 12).
- Stars in today's news told through a set story: Mammootty and Rajkumar Periasamy during the O.M release (World Filmology, Behindwoods "O.M title reason", Galatta "Major Mukund" story).
- Sundar C set stories (two in World Filmology's top 4).

## What this means for us (moved to rules/viral_factors.md)
1. Hidden-detail hooks on cult films beat technique-name hooks.
2. A director's own set story, checked in 2 sources and credited to the original interview, is our strongest untried angle for film-breakdown "behind the scenes" reels.
3. Tie a story to a film in this week's news (O.M now).
