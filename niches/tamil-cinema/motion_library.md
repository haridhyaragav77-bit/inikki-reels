# Motion library — the page's visual language

Motion graphics **show the idea the voice is saying, at the moment it is said**. They are not subtitles and not decoration (AGENT.md 5.8).

**Rules for every graphic**
- Labels of 3 words or fewer (names, numbers). Sentences are spoken, not written.
- Appears on the word it explains, leaves when the idea ends.
- Never twice in one reel (a single callback at the end is fine).
- The same idea always gets the same graphic, so viewers learn the page's language.
- One **hero moment** per reel: the richest graphic, layered, with depth, light and movement.
- When no clip fits a beat, the graphic goes full-screen and leads.
- Premium finish: smooth easing, depth layers, soft glow, film grain, brand colours. No clip-art.
- **Lots of animation.** Nothing sits still for more than a second. Every graphic enters, keeps moving and leaves with motion.

## Animation toolkit (used by every graphic)
| Moment | Moves to use |
| --- | --- |
| Enter | Pop with a small overshoot · lines that draw themselves · mask wipes · pieces that arrive one after another · blur-to-sharp · drop with a bounce |
| While on screen | Slow drift · gentle pulse · shimmer and light sweeps · floating particles and dust · a soft glow that breathes · numbers that keep ticking |
| The frame | Constant slow push-in (3–5%) · layers moving at different speeds (parallax) · a light camera sway on intense beats |
| Hit (big moment) | Flash · short shake · scale punch · colour burst · a ring of light pushing out |
| Leave | Whip out · shrink and fade · wipe away · break into pieces |
| Between screens | Whip pan · zoom through · light-leak flash · match move (one shape becomes the next) |
| Timing | Entrances and hits land on the music's beats; graphics arrive on the word the voice says |

The Content Specialist picks from this list. A new graphic is added here when an idea needs one, then reused. The weekly review notes which graphics hold attention best.

## Details, stories and sources (Harish, 19 Nov: from studying Expert FX and World Filmology)
Breakdowns now lead with one specific detail or a true story (AGENT.md 5.7). These are the screens for that; in a plan they
are the Stage kinds `detail`, `chain`, `quote` and `source` (templates/README.md).
| Graphic | The idea it shows | How it moves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `detail_zoom` (kind `detail`) | The detail most viewers missed | A magnifying lens drifts over the still as if searching, then glides to the detail on the word, opens wide and magnifies it; the rest of the frame darkens; a label hangs off the lens; a ring bursts on arrival |
| `detail_counter` (in `detail`, `count`) | Which detail of how many (hidden-details reels) | "DETAIL 2/3" at the top; one bar per detail, this one fills with a pop |
| `butterfly_chain` (kind `chain`) | One small choice leads to the ending (cause and effect) | Cards drop in one by one down the screen, each bigger than the last, joined by a running dashed thread; a tiny butterfly flaps on the first; the last card glows as the payoff |
| `insider_quote` (kind `quote`) | Who said it and where (behind-the-scenes stories) | A portrait in a glowing ring with voice bars pulsing round it like speech; the name slams, the role pops, a short key phrase rises word by word; the source tag (interview, year) lands last |
| `source_vs_film` (kind `source`) | The source story and the film side by side (a myth, a play, a real event) | Two tracks draw down the screen; beats pop in on each side; a bright curve with a running spark links the beats that match, and the pair lights up |
| `part_badge` (plan `part: [n, 3]`) | A film week's part | "PART 1/3" pill on the title and end screens |
